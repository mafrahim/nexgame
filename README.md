# 🎈 Kids Motion Playground (Nex-Style)


## 🎮 Playground Games Suite (11 Active Motion Games!)

1. **🐶 Puppy Bounce**: Lean side-to-side to guide the puppy, wave your hands to swat balloons with floppy proportionate paws, and physically jump to make the puppy leap up and bounce high balloons!
2. **🍉 Fruit Slasher**: Wave your arms like laser swords (wrist tracking) to slice flying melons and bananas into physically dividing slices while dodging spikey fuses bombs.
3. **🐹 Pat-the-Pets**: Reach high and low to pat cartoon bunnies, hamsters, and birds popping out of grass burrows, avoiding prickly hedgehogs.
4. **🚀 Cosmic Defender**: Stand back and lean left/right to revolve a glowing orbital neon space shield around your spaceship cockpit reactor, deflecting falling meteors away.
5. **🏒 Neon Air Hockey**: Smack glowing pucks with 1P vs Cyber-Bot or 2P local 1v1 hand paddles!
6. **✈️ Sky Wings Flyer**: Spread your arms like wings and bank left/right through 3D canyon rings & rainbow turbo gates!
7. **🐑 Shaun's Farm Dash**: Jump hay bales, squat under clotheslines, and bounce on Shirley in 1P/2P farmyard co-op!
8. **🐑 Shaun's Flock Smash (Live AR)**: Full-screen mirrored webcam AR—bop popping sheep & two-hit armored pigs across all 4 barn edges without bopping the Farmer!
9. **🧤 Super Goalie Rush (*GoKeeper*-Inspired)**: First-person stadium keeper! Dive for corners, tame banana curveballs, catch blazing fireballs with both hands, dodge trick mud-bombs, unlock **Giant Golden Magnet Gloves (2x)**, and snap a **Live Champion Polaroid Selfie**!
10. **🌟 Starri Beat Stage (*Starri*-Inspired)**: Holographic synth-pop rhythm concert! Catch beat gems, slash lightning diamonds, hit symmetric **Twin Harmony Chords**, dodge red Glitch Spikes, sprout **Cyber-Star Wings** in 4x Star Fever, and climb the `C` → `SS` Rank Ladder!
11. **🐿️ Copycat Mirror Party (*Elmo Says / Mirrorama*-Inspired)**: Match game-show host **DJ Acorn** in the Broadway AR mirror—Hands High, Super Wings, Laser Squat, Trampoline Jump, Spotlight Leans, **Red-Light Freeze Statue**, Lightning chains, and wearable **AR Costume Unlocks**!
